Vaani — privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who uses it

Vaani is a private tool used only by its owner, Sanjeev R. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Vaani accesses the Google Calendars of the owner's own Google accounts. It uses that access only to show his events to him and to create, update or remove events he has confirmed, with reminders. It never invites other people or sends anything on his behalf.

How data is used and kept

  • Calendar data and the messages he shares with Vaani are processed only to manage his own calendar.
  • Data is stored on a private server controlled by the owner. Shared screenshots are deleted 30 days after the event.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Vaani's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page.
