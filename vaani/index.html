Vaani

Vaani is a private personal assistant built for, and used only by, its owner, Sanjeev R. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

It helps him keep track of his own meetings and events: it reads messages he chooses to share with it and adds the meetings to his own Google Calendars, with reminders.
